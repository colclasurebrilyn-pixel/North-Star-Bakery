A neighborhood bakery with a slow-baked heart

Our story

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: make dependable, delicious bread and create a welcoming place for neighbors to meet. We bake early each morning so the shelves are ready when the community wakes up.

How we source

We choose seasonal ingredients and work with nearby growers and millers whenever possible. Our menu follows what is fresh, and we make each batch with care rather than rushing the process.

Meet the team

Head baker Mara leads our naturally leavened bread program, while pastry lead Luis brings local fruit and classic techniques together. Our counter team is happy to help you choose a loaf or plan a celebration.
